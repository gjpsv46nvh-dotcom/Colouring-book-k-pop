About Your Money AU

Your Money AU is a personal planning calculator for comparing pay, household budgets, mortgage scenarios, superannuation and investing.

Figures are estimates only. Tax, payroll, super, mortgage and investment outcomes can differ because of individual circumstances, fees, lender methods and changing rules.

Privacy

This beta stores the figures you enter in your browser on this device using local storage. It does not require an account or intentionally send your financial entries to a Your Money AU server.

Clearing browser/site data or changing devices can remove locally stored information. Avoid entering passwords, bank account numbers, tax file numbers or other identity credentials.

Important disclaimer

Your Money AU provides general calculation tools only and does not provide personal financial, tax, credit or investment advice. Consider checking important decisions against official information or an appropriately qualified professional.
